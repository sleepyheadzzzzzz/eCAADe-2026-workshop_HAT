# Workshop introduction

## Human–AI Teaming

The workshop guides participants from environmental evidence to an explainable
surrogate model and then reconnects that model to a design workflow. The urban
tree carbon-growth case provides the complete exercise; related examples show
how the same method can support thermal comfort, construction carbon, urban
vitality and spatial safety studies.

## Workshop facts

- **Dates:** 7–8 September 2026
- **Format:** hybrid; the second day is optional
- **Capacity:** 15–20 participants recommended, 30 maximum
- **Tutors:** Pia Fricker, Chaowen Yao, Yichao Shi and Zubin Tan
- **Primary tools:** Google Colab/Python, Rhino/Grasshopper and ONNX

## Curriculum

1. Surrogate model introduction
2. Feature extraction and data preparation
3. Model training, validation and test
4. Explainable environmental patterns with SHAP
5. Model encapsulation and ONNX export
6. Design support and spatial diagnosis
7. Optional agentic and multi-objective optimization

## Schedule

Day 1 introduces the method, develops the urban-tree case and begins the guided
exercise. Day 2 supports participant adaptation, discussion, peer review and
feedback. The exact room, Zoom link and final session timing are **To be
published soon**.

## Expected outcomes

Participants will be able to prepare an environmental dataset without leakage,
train and evaluate a transparent surrogate model, interpret environmental SHAP
evidence, export an ONNX model and reconnect its outputs to design decisions.
